# ❄️ Elemental Maces: Frost Mace (Minecraft Java 1.21.11)

Custom Datapack & Resource Pack adding the legendary **Frost Mace** with custom pixel art, crafting recipe, and 6 unique PvP abilities.

## ✨ Features & Abilities
- **Frost Armor (Passive in Hand):** Grants Resistance I and a snowflake aura.
- **Ice Meteor (Passive in Air):** Surrounds the player with a dense frost & end-rod vortex while falling.
- **Glacial Launch (Mainhand + Shift):** Launches the player high into the air for deadly mace slams.
- **Absolute Zero (On Hit / Slam):** Freezes the target (`TicksFrozen`), inflicts Slowness V & Weakness II, turns a 2x2 area under the target into Blue Ice with cobwebs, and unleashes a freeze shockwave.
- **Ice Cataclysm (10% Chance on Hit):** Launches all enemies within 20 blocks into the air, deals massive freeze damage, applies Blindness & Wither, and grants the user Strength II, Speed II, Regeneration II & Absorption II for 15 seconds.
- **Emergency Escape (In Inventory at ≤ 1 Heart):** Triggers Regeneration II & Speed II for 5 seconds to escape lethal situations.

## 📦 Installation (Zalith Launcher 2 / Java 1.21.11)
1. Download `FrostMace-Datapack-1.21.11.zip` and `FrostMace-ResourcePack-1.21.11.zip` from **Releases**.
2. Place `FrostMace-Datapack-1.21.11.zip` into `.minecraft/saves/<YourWorld>/datapacks/` and run `/reload`.
3. Place `FrostMace-ResourcePack-1.21.11.zip` into `.minecraft/resourcepacks/` and enable it in Resource Packs settings.
